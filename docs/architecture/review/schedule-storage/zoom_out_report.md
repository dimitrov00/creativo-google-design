# Stage 5 — Initial Zoom Out

Target: `docs/architecture/schedule-storage-and-lazy-evaluation.md`, lines
1–292. Reviewer: egm-zoom-out (fresh context), standard
`zoom-out-standard.md`. Goldfish context: `CLAUDE.md`,
`decisions/2026-11-06-breaks-live-on-the-roster.md`.

**Verdict: not ready.** The objective is clear (a store-or-compute rule, and
removing waste that grows with use). But:

- the rule breaks itself on the one case it exists to prevent;
- C4 and C5 put correctness at risk to save cents;
- C7 builds a dead end;
- the simplest, largest fix for the week view is marked out of scope.
