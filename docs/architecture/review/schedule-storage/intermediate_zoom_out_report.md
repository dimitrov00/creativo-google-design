## Recommended shape

1. **Problem and answer.** The split stays, and the document says why. Figures are estimates.
2. **Goals and non-goals.** Add one non-goal: tuning waits for the audit's post-deploy evidence.
3. **Terms.** About six: chair, roster, day change, projection, horizon, B.
4. **The data today.** The inventory, with a line of rough sizes.
5. **What one action costs.** Fix the `/book` row and state the horizon.
6. **The rule.** A truth clause and a derived clause, the table split into truth and derived, the ended repeat as a recorded exception, and a proposed Law 3 sentence for the owner.
7. **Compliance.** Three lines, for Laws 3, 5 and 6.
8. **Changes now.** C1, capacity only. Fix the stale `barberBusy` rules comment. Ask the owner about the F-25 mismatch.
9. **Waits for evidence.** C2, C3 and the per-barber capacity shape, each with its trigger.
10. **Dropped**, each with a one-line reason: C4, C5, C7, the busy half of C1, the sweep restructure.
11. **Alternatives and risks.** One register, covering only the changes that remain.
12. **Plan.** One phase: the `CapacitySystem` change and its spec, plus the comment fix, gated.
