Inside a sheet the same element is a module heading or a list label (uiHeaderProminence), with one trailing action that stays inside the column. ≙ SwiftUI .headerProminence(.increased) — a module heading inside a sheet. The title is title3 (20/600), one rung over the headline of the card it heads. EVERY heading is the small rung (36) tall — with an action, with a detail or alone — and stands one compact rung (8) over its body, so a page of modules keeps one rhythm. The cards carry the glyphs; a heading takes uiIcon only where no card follows. Never uppercase, never tracked.
