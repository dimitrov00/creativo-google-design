UiMark + ui-mark-scale — one mark grammar for every drawn range: the slider's named points, a gauge's notches and words, the nodes of a steps track. A mark is a value, a label (null = a notch without words), an optional short for inside a node and up to two detail lines. The scale writes the words under a range, each at its point. The loyalty page's catalogue: ui-gauge cuts a notch at each cost and hands its labelled marks to the scale — the word, then the detail line under it.

A word at ratio r steps back r of its own width: the first starts where the range starts, the last ends where it ends, a middle one centres on its point. Nothing hangs past the track, and nothing is measured in script. uiMinGap (a share of the range, 0.12 by default): a word closer than that to the previous one SHOWN is dropped, so the scale never overprints. The same pure rule is exported as visibleMarks(); the owner's value text still says every value.
