The one search field (ui-search-field): a glyph, a field, a clear. It can hold removable chips before the text — the filters the owner keeps beside what was typed. Pressing a chip, or Backspace in an empty field, takes it off. No chips: the field as the visit sheet's lists use it. Chips sit inside the frame before the text; the frame wears the field's fill, hairline and focus glow. Press a chip to remove it. Chips and text wrap together; the clear stays at the trailing edge.
