ui-date-field — segmented calendar-date entry: the GOV.UK date-input skeleton (fieldset + legend, three individually labeled numeric inputs, autocomplete="bday-*") wearing ui-otp-field's slot language. Auto-advance fires only on a complete segment, Backspace in an empty one moves back, and a pasted DD.MM.YYYY/DDMMYYYY distributes. Live fields — type or paste into them. value is {{ '{' }}day, month, year{{ '}' }} | null, non-null only while all three segments are complete; whether they form a REAL date is the consumer's domain VO, reported back through error.

value={{ format(defaultValue()) }}

value={{ format(filledValue()) }}

error="…" (consumer-decided)

Segment order, labels and placeholders are inputs — locales differ, so the DS hardcodes nothing (English defaults only, same policy as the phone picker chrome).
