ui-pass-card: what a client holds with the shop, drawn as the card in their wallet. A solid accent field with the balance as its one display figure, over a white foot with the holder in ordinary ink, inside one radius. One per page, and the page's one orange slab. Static: a group with one name (uiLabel). Slots: [uiPassMark], [uiPassBadge]. On a 390 pt phone the card is 355 wide and an ISO card tall (1.586): the field takes 62 % of the height, the foot 38 %. Only display sizes stand on the orange: the figure at 36/800 and its unit at 20/700, on one baseline. The mark is the shop's ui-logo; the ONE plate is a span[uiBadge] with uiOnMedia, the tier.
