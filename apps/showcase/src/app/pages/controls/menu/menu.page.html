ui-menu ≙ SwiftUI Menu / UIKit UIMenu — the anchored action list HIG reaches for by default. It opens next to the control that summoned it, doesn't dim the background, and light-dismisses on any outside press. The other tool is the action sheet, reserved for destructive confirmations where the finger travel is the point; a menu is not that. Trigger and items are both projected, so the menu owns anchoring, keyboard and dismissal while the consumer keeps its own control. A trigger in the [uiMenuTrigger] slot and any number of [uiMenuItem] commands. The trigger keeps whatever look the consumer gives it; the menu only stamps the APG aria-haspopup/aria-expanded relationship. Choosing any item runs it and closes the menu.

 A destructive command is red and goes last — the menu convention. (An action sheet is the mirror image: destructive first.) Reserve a confirmation step for actions that lose work the user typed; something one tap can undo should just happen.

 uiPlacement="automatic" (the default) flips the surface above the trigger when it wouldn't fit below, and the growth origin follows. uiAlignment pins the surface's leading edge, trailing edge or centre to the trigger — trailing is the right choice for a control sitting near the end of a row. The APG menu-button model: focus moves into the menu on open (no visible ring for pointer users — items style :focus-visible only), ↑/↓ cycle with wraparound, Home/End jump to the ends, Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger, and tabbing out light-dismisses. While closed the surface is visibility: hidden, which keeps every item out of the tab order without any inert bookkeeping.
