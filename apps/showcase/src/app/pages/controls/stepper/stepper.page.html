ui-stepper — journey wayfinding for short, fixed flows (the auth journey's identify → code → profile). Thin capsule segments: completed + current fill accent, upcoming stays on the hairline fill, the current segment carries a subtle width emphasis. A role="progressbar" over the step ordinals; the optional uiLabel caption doubles as the accessible name. Not a loading indicator — that's ui-progress-view. Caption above a full-width track — the stepper is sized by its surroundings (a toolbar slot's frame, a form column) and never asserts its own width.
