The team is one list

Everyone who works here, in the shop's own order: a face, a name and one quiet line that says how the person stands. The line holds the standing first and at most one note after it. The one filled disc on the screen adds someone. A content editor reaches the same list for the profiles: the same rows, with no accounts, no way to add and no «Права».

A member's page is their checklist

The five things a barber needs before anyone can book them are the first rows of the page, each a way into its own editor. Nothing asks the owner to press «go live»: the person starts taking bookings once the five are there and the start day has come. «Това съм аз» is the owner's door to their own chair; press it to see the row it leaves.

Adding someone takes three answers

Who they are, their name and the day they start. A tile is the answer: it fills the defaults a barber, a course student or a guest gets, and every default can still be changed on the member's page. Prices can be copied from a colleague so the first day does not begin with an empty list.

Permissions: a level, then its rows

«Права» lists the three levels the owner can edit, who holds each and how far it has moved from the standard. A level's page asks one question a row. On «Бръснар» the answer is a value, «Не», «Само своите» or «Всички»; everywhere else it is a switch. A changed row carries an orange dot and says what the standard was. Change a row: «Запази» comes into the dock, and the count on «Права» follows the save.
